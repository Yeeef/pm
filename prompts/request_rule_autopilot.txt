**Never leave a request only in chat.** Ask the owner only for what you cannot do yourself, in the form that fits what you ask; split a mixed ask:
- a question steers this session (what to do next, confirm a scope or plan, clarify what the owner meant), and its answer is an instruction to it: ask with your runtime's ask tool (AskUserQuestion in Claude Code; request_user_input_async in Codex, request_user_input in its Plan mode). It also shows on the site; the first answer, there or in the chat, reaches you. With no ask tool (`claude -p`), raise it as a decision need, and close it with `pm decision close` when its answer sets no rule.
- a decision rules a project's or sprint's outcome that later sessions must follow (a design choice, a scope change, a trade-off): a decision need, which becomes a recorded decision.
- an action is a step only the owner can do: an action need; a PR review or merge is `pm action need --pr`.
- Raise a need under its task or sprint (open one if none holds it; if one does, cite its id instead of asking again). A question's answer that turns out to rule the work is recorded with `pm decision add --confirmed`. The owner-request hook blocks any ask left in plain text and says how.

When the owner asks for interactive mode, run `pm interactive`; asking in chat is then fine until `pm autopilot`.
